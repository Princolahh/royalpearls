About us

The story and spirit behind Royal Pearls.

Who we are

Royal Pearls is a preschool in Pumula South, Bulawayo. Our name reflects how we see each child: as a pearl, precious and unique, growing into something beautiful with patient care. Our motto is Go. Grow. Glow.

More about our story, values and team will be added here soon.

Meet our director

Mrs T. Ncube

Mrs T. Ncube is the director of Royal Pearls. She is a qualified educator with a strong background in educational leadership and management.

  • Diploma in Education (United College of Education)
  • Bachelor of Educational Administration (University of Zimbabwe)
  • Master's in Educational Administration (Zimbabwe Open University)
  • Grow Your Business Certificate (WITS University)
  • ECD Management Certificate (IQ Academy)

Under her leadership, Royal Pearls is guided by one simple promise: Go. Grow. Glow. She is committed to a school where every child is treasured, well cared for and encouraged to reach their potential.
